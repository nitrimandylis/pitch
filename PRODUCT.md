# pitch

A folder of one-page pitch sites, plus the `/pitch` skill that produces them.

The point: when I need to sell someone on something (a crew for a hackathon
season, a tool for Dad, a side quest), I want a link, not a paragraph in a
group chat. The skill grills me until the pitch is actually a pitch, hallmark
builds it, GitHub Pages hosts it.
