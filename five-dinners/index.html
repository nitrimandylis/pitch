Five dinners for one evening. Four are Michelin tasting menus, one is not. Every course below is listed as the restaurant writes it, so nobody has to find out at the table.

Tasting menus are fixed: you eat the courses in the order given, and the whole table takes the same menu. The prices are per person, food only, before wine or pairings.

The △ marks a course built on something people commonly push away: offal, oily or cured fish, raw shellfish, game. Count your own. Four out of nine is a different evening from one out of nine.

Before booking

The date
Wednesday 9 September 2026. Midweek, so the tables are easier than a Saturday.
Dislikes
Every one of these kitchens asks for allergies and dislikes when you book, and swaps courses accordingly. Sending a list is a normal request, not a difficult one.
The menus move
Hytra and Soil both say their menus change constantly. The style holds; the individual courses in September will not all be the ones above.
Deposits
Delta €100 per booking, refundable 72 hours ahead. Soil €60 per person on Fridays and Saturdays, non-refundable but movable. Hytra, Spondi and Saipan: none stated.
Dress code
None of the sites read for this page states one. Ask when you call. It is a normal question and better answered before the night than at the door.
